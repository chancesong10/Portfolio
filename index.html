Snoozy

Live on Google Play
When
Sep 2026
Platform
Android 8.0+
Stack
Kotlin, Android Accessibility Service

Puts Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts to sleep, and leaves the rest of each app alone.

Open a Reel and Snoozy sends you back to the screen you came from, with a small note that Reels are snoozing. Your feed, DMs, stories, profile, search and regular YouTube videos all keep working.

  • Blocks only the scroll. Detects the Reels and Shorts players and backs out of them, muting the Reel for the moment before it closes.
  • Snooze timer from 15 minutes to 24 hours that can't be turned off early, with custom lengths in hours and minutes.
  • Reels in Chats. Watch the one Reel a friend sent in a DM or group chat. Swiping to more stays blocked. Allow or block each chat.
  • Private by design. No account, no ads, no analytics, and no internet permission, so nothing can leave the phone.

Ante

In testing
When
2026 – now
Role
Software Engineer
Platform
iOS & Android
Stack
React Native, Expo, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Jest

A bankroll tracker for gamblers, with a statistician built in.

Casinos know your numbers to the cent, but most players only have a feeling. Ante logs every hand, bet, buy-in and cash-out as it happens, then does the arithmetic. It never touches real money or connects to a casino.

  • Six game trackers for blackjack, poker, sports betting, roulette, baccarat and general play. Poker walks every street per player with a fully derived pot and won't advance on mismatched bets.
  • Session ledger with per-session ROI, win rate, profit factor and a bankroll-over-time chart that replaces manual spreadsheet tracking.
  • Leak detector that surfaces patterns memory hides: loss chasing from post-loss bet sizing, tilt, session fatigue and conditional win rates, each gated by sample size.
  • Offline-first sync. Fixed a silent bug that dropped synced sessions between accounts by rebuilding Supabase sync with encrypted local storage and integration tests that reproduced the clock-skew failure.
  • 240 Jest tests across 14 suites that caught state-sync race conditions in multi-session and multi-account flows before release.
  • Responsible gaming. Tiered stop-loss alerts that can't be swiped away, with a helpline link.

LearnIO

HackCamp build
When
2025, built in 24 hours
Platform
Desktop
Stack
Python, TypeScript, FastAPI, Electron, SQLite, Tailwind, Gemini API

A study toolkit that turns a syllabus into a practice exam.

Drop in a course syllabus. LearnIO pulls out the course and its topics, finds past exams online, extracts their questions into a local question bank, and builds a practice test filtered to the topics you pick.

Syllabus→Topics→Past exams→Question bank→Practice exam
  • Working end to end within HackCamp's 24-hour window, with an Electron front end and a FastAPI backend tying together Gemini, PDF processing, web search and SQLite.
  • From hours to 30 seconds. Finding practice exams went from hours of searching to about 30 seconds by downloading PDFs and running Gemini extraction concurrently with asyncio, using a semaphore and exponential-backoff retries to stay inside free-tier rate limits.
  • 1,000+ exam questions parsed, classified and indexed by automated Python scraping pipelines.
  • Also generates flashcards and keyword lists from the same backend.

Who plays the most?

Group research report
When
Nov – Dec 2025
Data
197 players, UBC PLAI Minecraft server
Stack
Jupyter, k-nearest neighbours

Can you predict how long someone will play from who they are?

With three teammates, I analysed player data from a Minecraft research server run by UBC's PLAI group.

  • Wrangled and cleaned the players and sessions datasets, dropping empty and identifying columns.
  • Found that nearly half the players had zero hours logged, and excluded them so the model wasn't skewed toward non-players.
  • Built a KNN classifier to test whether age and experience level predict play time, then reported where the model did and didn't hold up.
